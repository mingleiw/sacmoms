Holiday picks for Thanksgiving weekend and December

The holiday calendar fills up fast. Here are the family events around Sacramento we would put on the fridge now.

Effie Yeaw Holiday Market, Saturday Nov 28. The nature center's annual market of handmade, local gifts, with warm drinks, in Carmichael. An easy Thanksgiving-weekend outing with a walk on the trails after. Event page

Winter Wonderland at Fairytale Town, Dec 5 to 6 and 12 to 13. Santa visits, a Grinch appearance, crafts, a scavenger hunt, holiday lights at dusk and a snowfall each evening. It is ticketed, under 2s are free, and grown-ups need to bring a kid to get in. Event page

Kids' Night Out: Cocoa and Cookies, Friday Dec 11. A drop-off evening at Fairytale Town with crafts, games and dinner for kids 4 and up, so the grown-ups get a night off. Registration is required and space is limited. Event page

Noon Year's Eve at the Sacramento Zoo, Dec 31. On the zoo's events list now; check back there for details. Zoo events

One heads-up for Thanksgiving Day itself, Nov 26: the Sacramento Children's Museum, MOSAC and Fairytale Town are all closed. More seasonal events show up in the Special events section of the Sacramento page as they are announced.
