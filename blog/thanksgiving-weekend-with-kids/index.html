Thanksgiving weekend with kids: what's on, Thursday to Sunday

Thanksgiving is tomorrow, and a four-day weekend with kids needs a plan beyond the table. Here is what is on around Sacramento from Thursday morning to Sunday, and what is closed.

Thanksgiving morning: a family turkey trot. Run to Feed the Hungry in East Sacramento is the big one, a loop from J Street by Sac State through tree-lined East Sac streets, with a 10K and a 5K that you can walk. The race FAQ says strollers are welcome if they start in the walking corral, and dogs stay home. Race day info In Folsom, the Folsom Turkey Trot is billed as a family fun run, starting from the Rodeo Park and Lions Park area on Stafford Street. There is no sign-up on race morning, so check its site today if you still need a spot. Folsom Turkey Trot

Open on Thanksgiving Day: the Downtown Sacramento Ice Rink. The rink at Ali Youssefi Square on K Street keeps shorter holiday hours on Thanksgiving, which makes it a good way to burn energy before dinner. The smallest rental skate is a youth size 8, and the rink suggests age 3 and up. It is outdoors and closes in heavy rain, so call ahead if the sky looks grey. Ice rink page

Closed on Thanksgiving Day. The Sacramento Zoo, Fairytale Town, the Sacramento Children's Museum and MOSAC are all closed on Thursday, Nov 26.

Friday: the museums are back. The Children's Museum reopens for play on Friday, and MOSAC is open all through Thanksgiving week apart from Thursday. Fairytale Town is open Friday to Sunday, weather permitting, and the zoo is back to its usual schedule. Children's Museum · MOSAC

Friday night: Theatre of Lights starts its weekend run. Old Sacramento's light-and-story show plays on the rooftops and balconies of K Street on Friday, Saturday and Sunday nights from Nov 27 until Christmas Eve, with no show on Thanksgiving. It is outdoors, and shows can change with the weather. Theatre of Lights

Saturday: Dickens Street Faire in Old Town Elk Grove. Expect free kids' activities at the Old Town Plaza, photos with Santa, a fire department Santa rescue, carols and a story with Mr. and Mrs. Claus, and the town tree lighting on Walnut Street that evening. Event page The same day, the Effie Yeaw Holiday Market in Carmichael is on too, from last week's holiday picks.

If it rains. MOSAC and the Children's Museum cover a wet Friday, and our rainy-day plan has all four big indoor spots. Your city page lists more indoor places near you, starting with Sacramento.

Happy Thanksgiving from SacMoms. We are thankful for every parent who passes this site along.
